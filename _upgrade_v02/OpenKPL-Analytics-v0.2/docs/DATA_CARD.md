# Data Card

## Historical Draft Corpus
Verified: 5,586 rows; ordered `BP_process`; 10-slot `battle_process`; winner flags; hero, team, position and equipment fields. No trustworthy match/game ID, date, season, patch, player, KDA, gold or damage.

Permitted uses: descriptive draft analysis, role flexibility, composition analysis, retrospective draft classification, sequence modelling explicitly labelled non-temporal.

Do not make temporal, patch-specific, player-level or causal coach claims from this corpus alone.

## Temporal Series Backbone
Verified series-level fields include ID, season, stage, match time, teams, score, status and update time. This can support chronological team-strength models but does not itself provide individual-game drafts or player boxscores.
